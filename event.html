About Dance Club Meet

Dance Club Meet 2026 is a college cultural event organized for students who are interested in dance, music and stage performances. The event gives students an opportunity to show their talent and learn different dance styles from experienced performers.


Students can take part in solo, duet and group performances. The program also includes dance workshops, fun activities and a special performance by the college dance team.
